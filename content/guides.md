# Guides

## How to look for apartments

1. Define your budget (rent + utilities + transportation).
2. Choose 2–3 neighborhoods and list non-negotiables.
3. Use multiple listing sources and set alerts.
4. Prepare documents (ID, pay stubs, references) to apply quickly.
5. During tours, test water pressure, check for odors, and confirm included utilities.

## Negotiating rent

- Offer to sign a longer lease for a lower monthly rent.
- Ask for a small repair or earlier move-in in lieu of a discount.
