# Lease Break Letter Templates

Use these templates as a starting point when requesting early termination. Keep records and send by email and certified mail if possible.

## Mutual termination request

Subject: Request for mutual lease termination

Dear [Landlord Name],

I am writing to request a mutual termination of the lease for [address]. Due to [reason — relocating, job change, medical], I need to vacate on [date]. I propose the following: [pay X rent, help find replacement tenant, forfeit deposit]. Please let me know if we can agree to these terms.

Sincerely,
[Your name]

## Termination for habitability/landlord breach

Subject: Notice of uninhabitable conditions and request for remedy/termination

Dear [Landlord Name],

On [date] I reported [issue]. The condition persists and affects habitability: [describe]. Under local law, landlords must maintain habitable premises. I request immediate remediation; if not resolved in [reasonable time], I may pursue lease termination or other remedies.

Sincerely,
[Your name]

## Safety-related termination (domestic violence)

Subject: Request for early lease termination for safety reasons

Dear [Landlord Name],

Due to documented safety concerns related to domestic violence, I request an early termination of the lease under applicable protections. I can provide documentation from an advocate or court order if needed. Please advise next steps for safely vacating and returning security deposit.

Sincerely,
[Your name]
